17. Feedback Form

If you choose to send feedback, you may provide information such as your name, email address, rating, feedback, suggestions, or other information you enter into the feedback form.

Feedback is submitted through Google Forms. Information submitted through the form is processed by Google according to Google's applicable privacy policies.

Providing your name or email address is optional unless the form specifically marks a field as required. You can submit feedback without providing those optional details.

Google may process technical information associated with your use of the form, such as information necessary to provide and secure the service.
