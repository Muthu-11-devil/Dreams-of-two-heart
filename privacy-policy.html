Privacy Policy

Dreams of Two Hearts

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dreams of Two Hearts handles information when you use the app and its associated website.

Dreams of Two Hearts is designed as a creative proposal, storytelling, memory, and personal expression experience.

2. Information You May Enter

Depending on the features you use, you may voluntarily enter information such as:

  • Your name or another person's name.
  • Proposal messages and letters.
  • Poems and stories.
  • Memories and future messages.
  • Dates used for time-capsule features.
  • Photos you choose to use in the app.
  • Feedback and suggestions.

This information is provided voluntarily by you to use the corresponding features.

3. Local Storage

Some information used by Dreams of Two Hearts is stored locally on your device using browser or app storage.

For example, information used by the Future Me Time Capsule may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the app or browser storage, or uninstall the app.

Dreams of Two Hearts is not intended to be a permanent backup service.

4. Photos

If you choose a photo for a proposal or another feature, the app uses that photo for the selected feature.

The app is designed to use selected photos locally and does not intentionally upload them to a Dreams of Two Hearts server.

If you choose to share a photo using your device's sharing features, the receiving app or service will handle that content according to its own privacy policy.

5. Music

The app may allow you to play supported music or audio from your device.

Music selected from your device is used for playback and is not intentionally uploaded to a Dreams of Two Hearts server.

6. Online Templates and Website Resources

Some proposal templates and website resources may be loaded from internet-hosted resources.

When your device requests an online resource, the hosting provider may receive ordinary technical information required to deliver the resource, such as an IP address, device or browser information, and request time.

Dreams of Two Hearts does not intentionally send private proposal messages, time-capsule memories, or locally selected photos to an online template provider merely because you view an online template.

7. Feedback Form

Dreams of Two Hearts provides an optional feedback form so users can send suggestions, bug reports, feature requests, ratings, and general feedback.

The feedback form may allow you to provide information such as your name, email address, rating, feedback, and other information you voluntarily submit.

Feedback is submitted through Google Forms. Information submitted through Google Forms is processed by Google according to Google's applicable privacy policies.

Providing your name or email address is optional unless the form specifically marks a field as required.

You can review Google's privacy information here: Google Privacy Policy

8. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The app does not require an account for its main features.

We do not intentionally request or collect passwords, contacts, precise location, health information, financial information, or government identification through the app's normal features.

The app does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising purposes.

9. Sharing of Information

Dreams of Two Hearts does not intentionally sell your personal information.

Information used by local features is not intentionally shared with other people or companies by the app.

However, when you voluntarily submit feedback through Google Forms or share content using another external service, that service processes the information according to its own policies.

10. Third-Party Services

Dreams of Two Hearts may rely on third-party services such as website hosting and Google Forms to provide certain features.

Third-party services may process technical information or other information that you voluntarily submit to them.

Third-party services are responsible for their own privacy practices.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app or browser storage, or uninstall the app.

Feedback submitted through Google Forms may remain in the developer's Google Forms response records according to the developer's retention practices and Google's service policies.
